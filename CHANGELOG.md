# Changelog

All notable changes to **LKHODRA (الخضرة والغلة المغربية)** are documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-06

### Added
- **28+ Moroccan Fruits, Vegetables, Tubers & Aromatic Herbs** with full trilingual localization (`ar` Moroccan Darija/Arabic, `fr` French, `en` English), scientific binomial nomenclature, and regional dialects across Oriental, Chamal, Centre, and Souss/South.
- **12-Month Moroccan Seasonal Harvest Calendar (`يناير`–`دجنبر`)** distinguishing Peak Harvest (`ذروة الجني`), Early/Late Season (`البكور والآخير`), and Out-of-Season storage status.
- **Clinical Health Benefits & Organ System Explorer** mapping produce to 9 physiological targets (`heart`, `immunity`, `digestion`, `blood_sugar`, `brain`, `liver`, `eyes_skin`, `bones`, `anemia_iron`), ORAC antioxidant scores, Glycemic Index (GI), bioactive phytochemicals, food synergy tips, and traditional Moroccan remedies.
- **Moroccan Agricultural Terroir & IGP/AOP Atlas** across 10 major farming regions (Berkane/Triffa, Souss-Massa/Chtouka, Saïss/Meknès-El Hajeb, High Atlas/Midelt, Drâa-Tafilalet, Loukkos/Gharb, Doukkala, Tadla/Béni Mellal, Taliouine, Haouz) with live Open-Meteo agrometeorological telemetry.
- **Souk Weekly Qoffa (قفة السوق) & Head-to-Head Produce Comparator** with in-season vs off-season MAD/kg savings calculator and 1-click WhatsApp/Clipboard shopping list export.
- **Authentic Seasonal Recipes & Natural Washing Protocol** plus Darija voice pronunciation engine.
